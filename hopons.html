CMH Bus - Hop-On Registration

Use this form to book single bus rides on specific dates — great for the occasional morning drop-off, an afternoon pickup, or a whole week of rides. Rides cost $15 per ride, per child (a "Both" AM+PM day counts as 2 rides).

Pick a date and whether you need AM, PM, or both, then click Add Date. You can add as many dates as you like before submitting — they'll all be included in one registration.

Payment: Once submitted, you'll receive a confirmation email with the total. Please e-transfer that amount to cmhbusexec@gmail.com.

Our bus company requires advanced notice of all riders who may be on the bus for insurance purposes. Failure to complete this form at least a day in advance may result in your child being denied boarding.
